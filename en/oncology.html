Where the journey begins

Start with medical records, not an airline ticket

For a cancer case, the medical team needs a clear picture of the diagnosis, previous examinations and treatment history. Well-organized records help the receiving medical institution understand the case and determine whether additional examinations or an in-person consultation may be required.

01 / DIAGNOSIS

Clarifying the Diagnosis

Medical reports, pathology or histology findings and other available results help the medical team understand the nature of the disease.

02 / STAGE

Assessing the Extent of Disease

CT, MRI, PET-CT and other investigations may be relevant when evaluating the clinical situation and planning further steps.

03 / HISTORY

Previous Treatment

Information about previous surgery, systemic therapy, radiation therapy and other medical interventions can be important for the reviewing doctors.

Preparation

Medical records that may be useful

The exact list depends on the diagnosis and the individual clinical situation. You do not necessarily need every document before contacting us — the first step is to understand what is already available.

1
Medical Summaries

Disease history, diagnoses, specialist reports and available hospital discharge summaries.

2
Pathology and Histology

Available reports from biopsy, surgery or other morphological examinations.

3
CT, MRI and PET-CT

Written reports and, where available, the actual imaging files in an accessible digital format.

4
Laboratory Results

Relevant and recent laboratory tests or other investigations related to the disease and general condition.

5
Treatment History

Information about surgery, medicines, radiation therapy, treatment dates and available medical summaries.

6
Medical Translation

Where necessary, coordination of medical record preparation and translation for communication with the medical side in China.

Patient Journey

From Kazakhstan to medical review in China

The exact pathway may vary according to the diagnosis and the response of the medical institution. From an organizational perspective, the process usually follows several stages.

01 Contact The patient or family contacts ChinaMed Kazakhstan.
02 Records We identify which medical records and examination results are already available.
03 Preparation The required format, translations or additional information can be clarified where necessary.
04 Medical Review Prepared materials can be submitted to the medical side for preliminary consideration where available.
05 Travel After coordination, the necessary organizational aspects of the trip and patient support can be arranged.
06 Return Home Medical records and recommendations from the treating institution are important for subsequent follow-up.
Treatment Plan

Treatment is determined by doctors, not by the coordinator

There is no single cancer treatment pathway that is appropriate for every patient. Medical decisions depend on the type of cancer, extent of disease, examination results, previous treatment, general health and other clinical factors.

Surgery Whether surgery is appropriate and what procedure may be considered are medical decisions made by qualified specialists.
Systemic Therapy Medicines and treatment regimens are selected by treating doctors according to the individual clinical situation.
Radiation Therapy The suitability and scope of radiation treatment are determined by medical specialists after clinical assessment.
Combined Treatment Depending on the case, doctors may consider a sequence or combination of different treatment approaches.
Second Opinion

Medical review before travelling to China

Where a medical institution accepts cases for preliminary review, available medical records may be submitted so that the clinical information can be considered before travel.

A preliminary review does not replace an in-person examination and does not automatically confirm a previous diagnosis or treatment plan. After reviewing the records, the medical side may request additional examinations, pathology materials or an in-person consultation.

Patient Rights & Legal Matters

Treatment abroad involves more than medical issues

Patients may face questions involving documents, agreements, payments, medical information, interaction with foreign organizations and the protection of their legal rights.

ChinaMed Kazakhstan coordinates the medical journey. Where an issue requires independent legal assessment, the patient may obtain separate legal advice. Legal services are not medical services provided by ChinaMed Kazakhstan and do not influence the medical decisions of doctors or healthcare institutions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about cancer treatment in China

How do I start arranging cancer treatment in China?
Start with the medical records you already have. You can send medical summaries, doctors' reports and examination results to ChinaMed Kazakhstan. After an initial review of the available materials, the next organizational step and any additional records that may be useful can be clarified.
Which medical records may be required?
The exact list depends on the diagnosis. Useful materials may include medical summaries, pathology or histology reports, doctors' conclusions, CT, MRI, PET-CT and other examination results, laboratory tests and information about previous treatment.
Can I obtain a second opinion from a doctor in China?
This depends on the particular medical institution and clinical situation. Where a hospital or specialist accepts records for preliminary review, ChinaMed Kazakhstan can help coordinate preparation and submission of the available medical information.
Does ChinaMed Kazakhstan choose my cancer treatment?
No. ChinaMed Kazakhstan is not a medical institution and does not diagnose or prescribe treatment. Decisions regarding diagnosis, additional examinations and treatment are made by qualified doctors and medical institutions.
Can the exact cost of treatment be known in advance?
The final cost depends on the diagnosis, examinations required, the selected medical institution and the treatment plan. After the medical information has been reviewed, it may be possible to clarify a preliminary scope of medical and organizational expenses.
Can you help with an interpreter and travel arrangements?
Depending on the agreed scope of support, ChinaMed Kazakhstan can coordinate medical document translation, communication, transfers, language support and other organizational aspects of the trip.
